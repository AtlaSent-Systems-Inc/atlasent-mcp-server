# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Assume good faith, especially with newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other community channel), and whenever someone is officially representing the project.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **conduct@atlasent.io**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior violates this Code of Conduct, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
